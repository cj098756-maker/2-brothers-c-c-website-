Meet the Shop

Our Story

Two Brothers Cards & Computers started the way a lot of good things do: two brothers who couldn't stop talking about the games they love and the machines they love to build.

One of us grew up shuffling Magic, Yu-Gi-Oh!, and Pokémon decks at the kitchen table. The other was always taking computers apart to see how they worked and putting them back together better. Eventually we figured we could do both under one roof and do it fairly.

Today we're a small, local, family-run shop. We give honest card valuations, build custom PCs around what you actually need, and fix problems without the big-box runaround. When you deal with us, you deal with us.
