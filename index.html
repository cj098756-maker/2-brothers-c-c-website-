Frequently Asked Questions

What is the turnaround time for custom PC builds?

If parts are in stock, we assemble, stress-test, and benchmark within 24 to 48 hours. If parts need to be ordered, typical turnaround is 3 to 5 business days.

How do you condition-grade trading cards?

We adhere strictly to standard TCGplayer grading criteria (Near Mint, Lightly Played, Moderately Played, Heavily Played). Graded slabs are verified through official serial numbers.

Do custom PC builds come with a warranty?

Yes! All 2 Brother's custom builds include a 1-year in-store labor warranty plus individual manufacturer warranties on all hardware components.
